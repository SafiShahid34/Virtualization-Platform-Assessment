# Virtualization Platform: Five-Year Cost and Risk Review

**[View the live report →](https://virtualization-platform-assessment.netlify.app)**

An anonymized portfolio case study on choosing where an organization's virtual desktops and servers should run after VMware's licensing changes.

## Overview

When Broadcom moved VMware to per-core subscriptions, many organizations had to decide whether to renew or move to another platform. This case study works through that decision for an on-premises environment that delivers virtual desktops to thin clients and remote users, alongside a set of server workloads.

## What it covers

- The current environment and why a decision was needed
- The requirements every option had to meet
- VMware compared with open-source, Microsoft and cloud alternatives
- A five-year cost model and the assumptions behind it
- Trade-offs beyond cost, and a recommendation

## Approach

Every option is held to the same requirements and costed on its most efficient route, covering licensing, hardware and storage. Prices come from published or reported sources. Estimates are labeled and tested in a sensitivity analysis.

## About the data

This is an anonymized version of an analysis I completed at a previous employer. Hardware, figures and identifying details have been changed; the method and reasoning are unchanged. Prices reflect public sources as of October 2026 and are not vendor quotes.
